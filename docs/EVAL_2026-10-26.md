## 6. cuVSLAM vs. ORB-SLAM3, stereo, drive to the goal (3 drives each)

Same drive every time (`cfg.seed = 0`, the Play tasks start at the map start), so the three runs of one system are
almost identical: they show the determinism, not the variance of a system. Error against GT in the start frame (the policy's
pose), mean and max, and evo APE with `-a` (start-aligned, mean):

| Map | System | Steps | xy error mean / max (m) | evo APE mean (m) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A | cuVSLAM | 537 | 0.47 / 1.09 | 0.23 |
| A | ORB-SLAM3 | 537 | 1.56-1.59 / 3.16-3.19 | 0.29-0.34 |
| B | cuVSLAM | 1525 | 0.19 / 0.25 | 0.054 |
| B | ORB-SLAM3 | 1525 | 2.2-2.6 / 2.8-3.2 | 0.19-0.32 |

The alignment hides ORB-SLAM3's offset (its error is mostly a scale / drift that grows with distance), so the
un-aligned error is the fairer one for navigation. These runs use the 100 m far clip; the 10-10 numbers
(cuVSLAM 0.57 m, ORB-SLAM3 3.4-5.0 m on Map A) used another setup and are not directly comparable.
